MENU PHOTOS
Photos are now added inside the app: log in as Petrus -> Menu -> tap an item -> Gallery or Camera.
(With Cloudinary set up they are uploaded there; otherwise they are saved in the database.)

Optional: you can still bundle a photo in the app by naming it after the item id here, for example
bv01.jpg (Milo), fr01.jpg (Frappe Chocolate), ma01.jpg (Matcha Latte). Ids are in lib/seed.dart.
Use your own photos, square, about 600x600 px, under 200 KB each.
